Guiding Thought

"It was not a holiday. It was freedom."

The farm is not a holiday park and not an event venue. It is being developed as a place that grows over time: calm, open, craft-based and financially careful. Each new phase should create usability, enable income and fit the character of the place.

What makes it special is the combination of stays, knowledge sharing, regional encounters and real workshops. Guests do not simply consume the place. They experience it, help shape it or deliberately withdraw.

Place

An existing farm with room to grow

The property covers around 5.3 hectares with a main building, an almost completed holiday house, stable and barn buildings, outbuildings and open areas. Development does not work against the place, but from what is already there.

Implementation

Slowly, resiliently, with purpose

Development follows a clear sequence: infrastructure, first rental readiness, encounters, year-round offers, then guest houses and consolidation.

Founders

Robert & Anna Walter

Robert contributes landscaping, plasterwork, carpentry experience, forging craft and a very high share of own work. Anna complements the project with cooking, nutrition advice, gastronomy, course leadership, hospitality and existing professional stability in Sweden.

This combination is the economic core: the farm can grow slowly because much of it is developed by the founders themselves and early income does not depend on one large opening.
